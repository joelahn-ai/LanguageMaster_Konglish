# Pronunciation acknowledgments

The pronunciations of bounciness and withholding were transcribed to IPA from the exact complete entries of CMUdict, the Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary, revision 74790861f652b15e4ac49015a90074ad62a27690. The corresponding source lines, phoneme sequences, and source attribution appear in the editorial reference records and card pronunciation notes. Include LICENSE-CMUdict.txt with every distribution of this content package and with its app deployment documentation.

The Korean explanations, example sentences and translations, usage notes, and contextual relation descriptions are project-authored. Other dictionaries are cited per card for lexical verification; their recordings are not distributed. Speech is supplied by the device. Moby Pronunciator was examined as a research lead only and no Moby transcription is included in this candidate.
